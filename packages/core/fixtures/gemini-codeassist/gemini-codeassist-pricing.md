# Gemini Pricing

> Source: https://cloud.google.com/products/gemini/pricing

## Gemini Code Assist

### License fees (monthly commitment)

- Standard: $0.031232877 / 1 hour
- Enterprise: $0.073972603 / 1 hour

### License fees (12-month commitment)

- Standard: $0.026027397 / 1 hour
- Enterprise: $0.061643836 / 1 hour

### Currency

If you pay in a currency other than USD, then the prices listed in your currency on Cloud Platform SKUs apply.

### Billing

All subscriptions are billed monthly.

### Free Trial

30-day free trial for up to 50 users. Available for both Standard and Enterprise.

### How to Purchase

You can purchase your licenses directly from the Gemini Admin console, or connect with our sales team.
