## iOS in-app purchase

Cursor plans can be purchased through In-App Purchase in the Cursor iOS app. Apple acts as merchant of record for these purchases.

iOS subscriptions are available in every region where Cursor is on the App Store, which is everywhere except mainland China.

In-app purchase supports the monthly Pro, Pro+, and Ultra plans only. Yearly billing and Teams plans are available through the web checkout.

Purchases are managed through your Apple ID settings; plan changes are managed at cursor.com/dashboard/billing after logging in.
