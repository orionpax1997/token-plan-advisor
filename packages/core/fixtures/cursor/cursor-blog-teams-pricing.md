# Teams pricing: usage pools, Premium seats, and annual billing — Cursor Blog

> 快照：https://cursor.com/blog/teams-pricing-june-2026（2026-09-07 抓取）

Published June 1, 2026

Today we are updating Teams pricing around two monthly usage pools: a first-party models pool and a third-party API pool.

Teams Standard is $40 per seat per month ($32 per seat per month on annual plans); Teams Premium is $120 per seat per month ($96 per seat per month on annual plans). Premium seats include 5x the usage of a Standard seat.

Third-party model requests on Teams and Enterprise incur the Cursor Token Rate of $0.25 per million tokens, including BYOK.
