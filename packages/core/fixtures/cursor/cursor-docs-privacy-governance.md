## Data residency

US-only data residency is available for Enterprise customers. US-only data residency incurs a 10% uplift on Model pricing.

EU + Iceland inference-only coverage is available on request.

## Data processing

Teams and Enterprise plans are covered by a Data Processing Agreement (DPA). The DPA does not apply to individual plans.

The sub-processor list is published at trust.cursor.com/subprocessors.

Models are hosted by the model provider, a trusted partner, or Cursor.
