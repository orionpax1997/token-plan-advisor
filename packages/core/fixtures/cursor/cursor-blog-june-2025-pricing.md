# A clarification on June's pricing changes — Cursor Blog

> 快照：https://cursor.com/blog/june-2025-pricing（2026-09-07 抓取）

Published July 4, 2025

Last month we changed Pro from a request-based plan to usage-based billing, and we did not communicate this change clearly. We are sorry.

Our messaging implied that "unlimited usage" was only for Auto and not all other models, but many of you read it differently. We are issuing refunds to affected customers.

For context: under the old plan, a typical subscriber could make about 225 Sonnet 4 requests, 550 Gemini requests, or 650 GPT 4.1 requests with the included $20 of model inference at API prices per month.
