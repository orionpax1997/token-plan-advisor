## Model availability by region

Some models are subject to region restrictions decided by the model provider. Depending on your location, certain models may not be available in your region. When this happens, those models won't appear in Cursor, but all other models continue to work. You can switch models, use Auto, or bring your own API key (BYOK).

Which models are affected depends on the model provider's supported-region lists.

## Cursor Start (India)

The Cursor Start plan is only available in India. Subscribing to Cursor Start requires verifying an Indian phone number.

If you access Cursor from outside India or use a VPN, your requests may be blocked.

## Data residency

Enterprise customers can request US-only data residency. See Privacy and Data Governance for details.
