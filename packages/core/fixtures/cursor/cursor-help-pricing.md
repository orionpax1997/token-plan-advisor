## Plans and pricing

| Plan | Price | Other Models usage |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hobby | Free | Limited |
| Pro | $20/mo | $20 |
| Pro+ | $60/mo | $70 |
| Ultra | $200/mo | $400 |
| Teams Standard | $40/user/mo | Standard team allowance |
| Teams Premium | $120/user/mo | 5x Standard team allowance |

All individual plans include unlimited tab completions, extended agent usage limits on all models, access to Bugbot, and access to Cloud Agents.

Hobby: Limited Agent requests, Limited Tab completions.

Usage resets monthly with your billing cycle. Unused usage does not roll over.

## FAQ

**What payment methods are supported?**
Self-serve plans support all major credit and debit cards. Enterprise customers can contact us for invoice-based billing and wire transfers.

**Can I buy Cursor through a reseller?**
Cursor subscriptions are only sold directly through cursor.com. We do not authorize any resellers or third-party sellers.

**Do yearly plans cost less?**
Yearly billing is available at checkout.

**Where do I manage my subscription?**
Log in and manage your plan at cursor.com/dashboard/billing (Stripe billing portal).
