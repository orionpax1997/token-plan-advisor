# Introducing Ultra and a new pricing model — Cursor Blog

> 快照：https://cursor.com/blog/new-tier（2026-09-07 抓取）

Published June 16, 2025

Today we are introducing Ultra, our most powerful plan yet, at $200 per month — 20x more usage than Pro.

We are also changing how Pro usage works. Pro previously included a limit of 500 requests per month, with Sonnet models costing two requests. Starting today, Pro moves to usage-based billing: your subscription includes $20 of model inference at API prices per month, consumed across the models you use.

Fast requests and slow requests are retired as part of this change.
