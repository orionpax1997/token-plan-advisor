## Privacy Mode

Privacy Mode ensures your code is never used for training by Cursor or other AI model providers.

Privacy Mode can be enabled per user, and team administrators can enforce it organization-wide. Privacy Mode is on by default for Enterprise teams.

## Zero data retention

Most models run under Cursor's ZDR agreements, which means prompts and outputs are not retained by the model provider after a request completes.

Models that require data retention (Claude Fable 5) are off by default for teams and require administrator approval before use.

## Terms of Service

Under the Terms of Service, ANYSPHERE WILL NOT USE CONTENT TO TRAIN, OR ALLOW ANY THIRD PARTY TO TRAIN, ANY AI MODELS, UNLESS YOU'VE EXPLICITLY AGREED.
