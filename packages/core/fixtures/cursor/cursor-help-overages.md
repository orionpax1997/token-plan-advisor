## On-demand usage

When your included pool is exhausted, you can enable on-demand usage to keep working. On-demand usage is billed at API rates with no markup.

On-demand charges are billed in arrears, at the end of each monthly billing cycle.

## Auto modes and cost

Auto has three modes: Cost, Balance, and Intelligence. Auto Cost pricing is set per million tokens, regardless of which model is used. Balance and Intelligence cost about twice as much as the previous Auto mode on average, and up to two to four times as much. The routed model identity is hidden by default.

## Teams

For Teams and Enterprise, third-party model requests also incur the Cursor Token Rate of $0.25 per million tokens.
