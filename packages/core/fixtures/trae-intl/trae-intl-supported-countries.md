# Supported Countries and Regions

> Source: https://docs.trae.ai/ide/supported-countries-and-regions

TRAE is currently available in the following countries and regions.

## Asia

Indonesia, Singapore, Malaysia, Philippines, Thailand, Vietnam, India, Japan, Korea, Myanmar, Laos, Cambodia, Brunei, Timor-Leste, Cocos (Keeling) Islands, Christmas Island, Bangladesh, Sri Lanka, Nepal, Maldives, Bhutan, Pakistan, Afghanistan, Mongolia, Saudi Arabia, United Arab Emirates, Bahrain, Yemen, Iraq, Oman, Israel, Palestine, Jordan, Qatar, Lebanon, Kuwait, Uzbekistan, Tajikistan, Turkmenistan, Kyrgyzstan, Kazakhstan, Turkey.

## Europe

United Kingdom, Germany, France, Spain, Italy, Netherlands, Belgium, Luxembourg, Switzerland, Austria, Sweden, Norway, Denmark, Finland, Iceland, Ireland, Portugal, Greece, Poland, Czech Republic, Slovakia, Hungary, Romania, Bulgaria, Croatia, Slovenia, Estonia, Latvia, Lithuania, Malta, Cyprus.

## North America

United States, Canada, Mexico.

## South America

Brazil, Argentina, Chile, Colombia, Peru, Uruguay, Paraguay, Bolivia, Ecuador, Venezuela.

## Oceania

Australia, New Zealand, Papua New Guinea, Fiji, Solomon Islands, Vanuatu, Samoa, Tonga, Kiribati, Tuvalu.

## Africa

South Africa, Egypt, Nigeria, Kenya, Morocco, Tunisia, Ghana, Senegal, Côte d'Ivoire, Ethiopia, Tanzania, Uganda, Rwanda, Mauritius.

## Antarctica

Antarctica (research stations only).

## Countries Not Yet Supported

Mainland China, Hong Kong SAR (China), Macao SAR (China), Taiwan, North Korea, Iran, Syria, Cuba, Belarus, Russia (under sanctions review).
