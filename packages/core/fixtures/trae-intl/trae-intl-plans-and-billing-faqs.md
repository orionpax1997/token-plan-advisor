# Plans and Billing FAQs

> Source: https://docs.trae.ai/ide/plans-and-billing-faqs

## When is my account charged?

Your account will be charged for automatic renewal one day before the current subscription period expires.

## What is included in my plan?

Each plan includes Basic Usage, Bonus Usage, and access to features such as AI chat, autocompletion, and concurrent cloud tasks. See the Plans and Billing page for plan-specific limits.

## Can I cancel my subscription?

Yes. You can cancel auto-renewal at any time before the next billing date. Your subscription will remain active until the end of the current billing period.

## How do I cancel auto-renewal?

1. Go to TRAE Console
2. Click Plan & Billings
3. Toggle off auto-renewal

## What happens to unused Basic Usage?

The unused quota for the current month will not be carried over to the next month.

## Can I request a refund?

Trial subscriptions may be cancelled within the trial period. Paid subscriptions are non-refundable except as required by applicable law.

## Why does the message indicate that the current region cannot be subscribed to?

TRAE provides subscription services only in certain countries and regions. For more information, refer to (New) Plans & billing.

## How can I switch plans?

You can upgrade at any time. Downgrades take effect at the end of the current billing period.

## What is Bonus Usage?

Bonus Usage is an additional flexible usage each month based on your actual use.

## Can I get a custom plan?

Currently, only TRAE CN supports enterprise procurement. Contact our sales team for Team / Enterprise plans.

## What models are available?

TRAE supports GPT, Claude, Gemini, ByteDance Seed, Moonshot Kimi, and other frontier models. Model availability may vary by region.

If the model you select (such as Gemini) supports an ultra-large context (for example, 1M tokens), a "Max" switch will appear.

## How does the Free Trial work?

New users can claim a Pro Trial within 48 hours of registration. The trial lasts 7 days. You must bind a credit card and enable auto-renewal.

## What is the difference between Pro and Pro+?

Pro+ provides 4.5x more Basic Usage than Pro, plus higher queue priority and faster cloud task concurrency.
