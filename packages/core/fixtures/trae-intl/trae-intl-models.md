## Region Restrictions

The following AI models are not available to users in the United States: GPT series and MiniMax series.

(Continued restrictions may apply; refer to the latest Models page for updates.)

## Custom Models

You can configure custom models via TRAE Console. Supported providers include Google, OpenAI, Anthropic, BytePlus, Volcano Engine, and 25+ others. Custom models do not consume Basic Usage.
