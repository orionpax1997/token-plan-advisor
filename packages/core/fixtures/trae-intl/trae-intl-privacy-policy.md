# Privacy Policy

> Source: https://www.trae.ai/privacy-policy
>
> Effective: 2026-01-22

## 1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly (account registration, payment information), and information collected automatically through your use of TRAE (usage data, log data, device information).

For US users and other US individuals, we collect code snippets and AI-generated outputs for the purpose of training and improving our technology.

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

- Provide, maintain, and improve TRAE
- Process payments and subscriptions
- Detect and prevent fraud
- Train and improve technology (for US users: code snippets and AI-generated outputs)

## 3. Codebase Processing

To enable codebase indexing, we temporarily upload your codebase files to our servers to compute embeddings, after which all plaintext code will be permanently deleted.

## 4. Privacy Mode (TraeWork)

When Privacy mode is enabled, TraeWork will not use any of your chat interactions, including related code snippets and AI-generated outputs, for analytics, product improvement, or model training.

Regardless of your Privacy mode setting, TraeWork will not use your codebase files for analytics, product improvement, or model training.

Privacy mode is only effective when your TRAE account is logged in.

## 5. Cloud Processing

For TraeWork Web and cloud agent features, your codebase files are processed and retained in the cloud.

## 6. Data Retention

- Account information: retained for the duration of your account
- Usage logs: retained for 90 days
- Code embeddings: deleted after computation

## 7. Your Rights

You may access, correct, or delete your personal information at any time through TRAE Console.

## 8. Contact

For privacy inquiries, contact privacy@mail.traeai.com.
