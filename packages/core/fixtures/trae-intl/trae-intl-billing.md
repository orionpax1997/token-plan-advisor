# Billing (Legacy)

> Source: https://docs.trae.ai/ide/billing
>
> Note: This page describes the legacy billing model. The new token-based billing model is now in effect. Refer to (New) Plans and billing for current plan information.

## Legacy Plan Model

Under the legacy billing model, TRAE used fast requests and slow requests as the unit of measurement:

- **Fast requests**: Processed with priority queue
- **Slow requests**: Processed with standard queue

## Legacy Plan Pricing

| Plan | Monthly Price | Fast Requests / Month | Slow Requests |
|------|---------------|------------------------|----------------|
| Free | $0 | 200 | Unlimited |
| Pro | $10 | 600 | Unlimited |

## Add-on Packs (Legacy)

- USD $3: 100 fast requests (30 days)
- USD $7: 300 fast requests (30 days)
- USD $12: 600 fast requests (30 days)

## Conversion (Legacy)

6 Fast Requests = $1 Dollar Usage.

## Migration to New Billing

The transition to token-based billing took effect on February 24, 2026 (2 AM UTC). All users have been migrated automatically. Fast request balances were converted to Dollar Usage at the rate above.

## Billing Cycle (Legacy)

Each month is calculated as 30 calendar days.
