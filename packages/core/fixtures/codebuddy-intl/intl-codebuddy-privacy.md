# CodeBuddy Privacy Policy (International)

> Service entity: Tencent Cloud International Pte. Ltd. (Singapore).

## Service Availability

"This Feature is available to users globally, but primarily intended for users located in the same country/region as the selected service region for optimal performance."

## Personal Data Storage

- Your personal information will be stored in servers in Singapore.
- Login and security credentials are stored in Hong Kong.

## Data Training

We do not store any code or project content permanently; data is processed only to provide functionality.

## Data Retention

- IP addresses: retained for 180 days
- Backend logs: retained for 14 days
- Account deletion: information deleted within 30 days of account deletion

## Cross-Border Access

Tencent's global support team, **including from the People's Republic of China (PRC), who may access your personal information**, supports the service.

## Children's Privacy

Users must meet the minimum age of consent in their jurisdiction (per DPSA).

## Service Agreement

The Service Agreement is signed with **Tencent Cloud International Pte. Ltd., a Singapore registered entity**; disputes are governed by Singapore law.
