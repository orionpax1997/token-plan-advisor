# CodeBuddy Billing Overview

Last updated: 2026-08-07 09:37:37

## Pricing Plans Effective August 7, 2026 (UTC+8)

When the pricing plan takes effect on August 7, 2026 (UTC+8), the CodeBuddy International Individual Edition will adopt the following pricing:

| Plan | Monthly | Annual | Total Credits / Month |
|------|---------|--------|-----------------------|
| Free | Free | Free | 100 |
| Pro | $10.00 / month | $96.00 / year ($8.00 / month, billed annually) | 2,000 (1,000 base + 1,000 bonus) |
| Team | $40.00 / seat / month | $480.00 / seat / year | 1,000 credits per seat per month, shared through a team credit pool |

## Price Retention

**Pro (monthly/annual) users who enabled auto-renewal before August 7, 2026 retain their subscription price of $9.95/month** and continue to use the Pro plan with the existing feature set until they cancel auto-renewal.

- Credit Card
- Debit Card
- WeChat

## Free Trial

Pro 7-Day Free Trial: after binding a credit card and enabling auto-renewal, 500 credits are granted (7-day validity).

## Credits Validity

Base and bonus credits are issued monthly and are valid for that month; they do not roll over.

## Service Entity

Service Agreement is signed with **Tencent Cloud International Pte. Ltd., a Singapore registered entity**.
