## New User Benefits

- New users receive 250 credits upon registration and login (valid for 14 days).
- Pro 7-Day Free Trial: after binding a credit card and enabling auto-renewal, 500 credits granted (7-day validity).
- After subscribing to Pro or starting the 7-day Pro trial, the Free plan's 100 credits are no longer issued separately.

## Promotional Bonus

Promotional Bonus is a limited-time offer. The end date will be notified separately.

## Billing Cycle

"Your billing cycle starts on the day of subscription."

"Base and bonus credits are issued monthly and are valid for that month; they do not roll over."
